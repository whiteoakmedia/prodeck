# Thank you
Thank you to every church running ProDeck, and to everyone who sent in a bug, an idea or a kind word. Your support is what keeps this going, and it's a joy to build it with you.
# New
- Step by step setup for multitrack recording: pick the source (Dante or a console over USB), check levels, name tracks, choose a drive.
- Step by step setup for Automix: your console, which of your faders plays each part, instrument feeds, backing vocals, song timing and speech mics.
- Automix now works with your desk's own fader names, so "Elec Gtr" works as well as "EGs".
- ProDeck is a free gift to every church from White Oak Media. You'll see that at the bottom of the sidebar now.
# Fixed

# New
- Send a message to a TV. In Messages, choose a screen as the destination: All screens, or one dashboard's screens, like the green room TV. It goes across those TVs in big type with a soft chime, and comes down after three minutes or when you press Clear.
- Update the TV app from the booth. Settings, TV screens now lists every TV with what it shows and its app version. Press Update or Update all: the TV downloads the new ProDeck Display and installs it over the top, keeping its screen. Someone presses Update on the TV's remote, then opens the app again. TVs set up before this version need the new app installed once with Downloader.
- Change what a TV shows from the booth: choose a dashboard next to the TV and press Show.
- The crew poster works any time. Scanning it while joining is closed used to be a dead end. Now people ask to join with a name and PIN, you approve them under Crew Members, and their phone is let in straight away.
- Dashboards on any TV with a Fire TV Stick or an Android TV box. Install the free ProDeck Display app (the steps are in Settings, TV screens): the TV shows a code, you pick a dashboard on the booth, and from then on the TV opens it by itself, stays on, and comes back after a power cut.
- Choose what a TV shows from the TV itself: press Menu or the centre button on its remote for every dashboard, the camera tally board, and an edge margin for TVs that crop the picture.
- A ProPresenter Macros tile: every macro in ProPresenter as a button, in the colour you gave it in Pro. Tap to run, and reorder or hide them while editing the dashboard.
- ATEM downstream keys and Fade to Black on the ATEM tile: each key lights red while it's on air and shows what it keys; tap to take it on or off. FTB asks before fading to black.
- An ATEM Macros tile: the macros saved on your switcher as buttons, with Stop and Continue. Stream Deck can run them too, by number or name.
- Camera tally on any phone or iPad. Settings, Camera tally makes a link and code for each camera: the screen goes red when that camera is live and green when it's next. Choose a single tally light, an operator view that also shows what's live and next, or a board of every camera for an iPad. Add it to the Home Screen and it opens full screen.
- ATEM switchers with more than one M/E: each ATEM tile picks its own M/E.
- Arrange the ATEM tile your way while editing the dashboard: drag sources into order, hide or rename them, and choose where Cut and Auto sit.
- ProPresenter view only. One switch in Settings makes ProDeck show everything ProPresenter is doing without ever changing it, for a second computer or a church that runs slides from Pro itself.
- Allen & Heath Qu-5, Qu-6 and Qu-7. Pick it in Settings, Connections, Sound Console. ProDeck mirrors its mutes, faders and scenes, just like an SQ. Thank you, Kasey, for asking for it.
# Fixed
- A page to everyone reaches the crew scheduled on this week's plan, even if they haven't opened ProDeck today. It used to reach only people who had checked in.
- The page composer shows who has notifications off, so you know who will only see a page with ProDeck open.
- Kiosks and TVs show the whole dashboard. A tall dashboard used to run off the bottom of a TV; now it scales down evenly to fit, and stays sharp.
- A TV that's paired again replaces its old code instead of being turned away after a few tries.
- Every dashboard tile now works in any shape: wide and short, tall and narrow, small, and on a phone. System Health, the ATEM Switcher, Song Key, Keys to the Stage, the SPL meter and Checklist rearrange themselves instead of squashing, overlapping or spilling.
- The TapLink tile could take the whole dashboard down if its tap counts came back in an unexpected shape.
- Service analytics decide which service it is by the clock. The last service of the day now ends by itself, and everything outside a service (rehearsals, run-throughs, the rest of the week) goes to that day's Practice report, so there's nothing to reset when Follow Pro stays on.
- NFC taps are counted once: only inside each service's own time, never during practice, and one tap per phone per minute, ignoring link previews.
- The ATEM tile on phones and kiosks now updates live. It used to change only after a reload or a button press.
- If the switcher drops, the ATEM tile keeps showing the last Program and Preview while ProDeck reconnects, and Settings shows how long the link has been up.
- Unused switcher inputs named "--" are hidden from the ATEM tile and tally screens.
- The Sound desk light names the console you picked. It used to say "Can't reach the Avantis" whatever desk you had.
- When the desk can't be reached, the light now says why, and reminds you that Companion or MIDI Control may already be holding the desk's MIDI connection.
- ProDeck gives up on an unreachable desk after five seconds and tries again, instead of waiting up to a minute.

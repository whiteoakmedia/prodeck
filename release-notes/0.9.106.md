# New
- A Settings page you can find your way around. Settings is now six sections (Connections, Sound, Automation, Online, Crew & screens, App & help), one at a time, instead of one long scroll.
- Find a setting: type in the search box and ProDeck looks through every word on every card.
- A bar appears as soon as you change something, with Discard and Save changes, and ProDeck asks before you leave Settings with changes unsaved.
- Crew & screens shows how many people are waiting to join your crew.

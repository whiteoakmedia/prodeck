# New
- Church Online Platform. Add your Church Online address in Settings and the new Church Online tile counts down to your online service and shows when it's live. Connect its webhooks too and the tile counts who watched and chatted, and lists prayer requests and salvation responses with names as they happen. Pick crew to page, and their phones buzz so someone can go and pray with them.
- Walk-in music. Control the Spotify or Music app on the booth computer from any dashboard or crew phone: what's playing, play and pause, skip, volume, Start walk-in and Fade out. It fades out by itself so it's silent as each service starts. Mac only for now.
- Stream Monitor (Resi). Paste your Resi embed code into the new tile and phones and kiosks show the stream as viewers see it, muted to start. It never plays on the booth computer, so its sound can't reach the house.

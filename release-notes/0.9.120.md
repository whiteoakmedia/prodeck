# New
- ProDeck Crew is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Volunteers install it, tap Scan crew code and scan your crew poster. It remembers your church and rings for pages with the phone locked.
- Your crew can reach ProDeck from anywhere, even with the booth computer off. Each church gets its own crew address, and phones switch to it by themselves when the booth doesn't answer. Away from church or with the booth off, volunteers still see this week's plan, call times, checklists and chat, and their checklist ticks and messages reach the booth when it's back. A copy of the plan, crew names, checklists and chat is kept on White Oak Media's servers for this, and phones away from church reach the booth through them. Passwords are only kept there as a scrambled check, never the password itself. It's on by default, and Settings, Crew & screens, Crew address has the switch.
- Pages you can't miss, with ProDeck Crew 1.1 (with Apple for review now). A page has its own alert sound and rings again with each repeat until it's confirmed. Swipe the notification left, tap View, then Got it, and it's confirmed without opening the app.
- A tidier Messages panel: who it's to and Page someone on one line, a full width box to type in, and messages that are easy to read. The booth now signs its messages as Booth.
- iPhones get a one time tip after turning on page notifications: on silent, a page only buzzes if Settings, Sounds & Haptics, Haptics is set to Always Play.
- What's new in ProDeck. After this update, the booth opens a short animated tour of everything below, with where to turn each thing on. Open it again any time from Help.
- Comms on every phone. Crew phones and iPads become an intercom with four lines: All, Cameras, Stage and Booth. Hold the big button to talk, or turn on Open mic and use Mute. Turn it on in Settings, Crew & screens, Comms. The booth computer never plays comms, so nothing reaches the house.
- Any phone is a tally light. On a crew phone, More, Tally light, then pick the camera: red when it's live, green when it's next. While it's a tally it makes no sound and never vibrates, and a page shows as a quiet strip across the top.
- A secure address in one click. Settings, Crew & screens, Secure local address: type a name and press Claim this address. Phones need it for comms, a camera's microphone and notifications. It stays inside your building, follows the computer if its address changes, and renews itself.
- Crew view on iPad: comms, the tally light and the phone tabs, sized for the iPad. Full view at the bottom of More switches back to every page and dashboard.
- Calmer crew screens: a calmer Home, every dashboard on the phone, More with icons, and a bottom bar you can tap anywhere. Tap the tab you're on to jump back to the top.
- Someone who already has an account can sign in with their name and PIN from a new phone, instead of being asked to join again.
- Support ProDeck. ProDeck is free for every church. The Support ProDeck link in the sidebar and in Help shows how to help keep it that way.
# Fixed
- A church turning on its crew address the first time can use it straight away. The booth could wait up to half an hour before connecting to it.
- A new crew address is named after your church from Planning Center, not after the booth computer.
- A page shows straight away when you tap its notification. It used to wait for the booth's next repeat, up to 20 seconds.
- With the ProDeck Crew app open on screen, a page now rings and shows a banner. It used to arrive silently.
- Phones, iPads and TV screens reconnect to the booth by themselves. If the booth or its internet had a hiccup, a phone could sit on "booth offline" until someone reloaded it, and a phone waking from sleep could keep a connection that had quietly died. Now they notice within a minute and come back on their own.
- One phone or TV with an old password can no longer lock everyone else out. A device still holding the crew password from before it was changed kept asking, and after a few tries ProDeck turned away every phone sharing its connection, even with the right password. Now only real guessing is slowed down.
- ProDeck no longer opens Service setup by itself every morning. It's still on the Planning Center page, under Service setup, whenever you want it.
- Dashboards on TVs fit the screen. On a Fire TV Stick, tiles like Mic Assignments, Live Viewers and the SPL meter ran past their boxes and showed scrollbars nobody can use with a remote. Now each tile draws its content a little smaller until it fits, short tiles keep just what matters, and Show Flow keeps the live item in view by itself.
- A TV stays on its dashboard when the booth computer comes back on a new address. It used to show "Webpage not available" until someone paired it again; now it finds the booth by itself within a minute. Press Update all in Settings, TV screens to get the new TV app.
- While the booth is away, a TV shows "Looking for ProDeck on the network" instead of an error page.
- The bottom bar on crew phones answers every tap. A tap near the edge of the Home button used to do nothing.
- ProDeck for Mac is now signed and checked by Apple, so it opens like any other app with no trip to Privacy & Security. After this update, macOS may ask once more for things you allowed before, like the Downloads folder or the microphone. Choose Allow.

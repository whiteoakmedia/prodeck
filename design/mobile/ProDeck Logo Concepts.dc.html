Notes for the ramp
Icon PNGs are full-bleed squares on #161d28 — let macOS apply the squircle mask. Wordmark SVGs carry live IBM Plex Sans SemiBold (OFL, embeddable); outline them before handing to a printer. Geometry is a 100-unit grid, single 10.5 stroke, so redraws stay exact. Author credit is set in IBM Plex Mono at 18% of the wordmark size, tracked +.2em, and is locked into every lockup; every file also carries dc:creator “Zach Green” in its metadata. Icon-only exports below 128 px omit the visible line — it is unreadable at those sizes — and carry the credit in metadata instead.

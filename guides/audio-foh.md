# Volunteer Audio FOH Operator

You help create a clear, distraction-free worship experience by managing the sound mix during services. You make sure worship leaders, musicians, speakers and media are balanced so the congregation and the online audience can fully engage.

You are responsible for the room mix, making thoughtful adjustments throughout the service. **No prior experience is required** — training and ongoing support are provided.

## What you'll do

- Operate the front-of-house console during services and events
- Mix vocals, instruments, speaking microphones and media playback
- Create a balanced, consistent mix for the room
- Follow cues from the Production Lead and worship leadership
- Work with musicians, worship leaders and production volunteers as a team
- Monitor and adjust levels throughout the service
- Keep audio clear for both in-room and online audiences
- Support special services, events and rehearsals as needed

## What you don't need to know yet

- You don't need previous audio engineering experience
- You don't need to understand advanced sound equipment
- You don't need to know how to mix a full band before serving
- You will receive training and support from the production team

## Time commitment

- **1 Sunday per month**, depending on scheduling — you can serve more if you'd like
- **Thursday nights at 6pm** for all-team rehearsal

## What success looks like

- The room sounds clear, balanced and consistent
- Vocals and instruments support worship without distraction
- Speakers are easy to understand
- Volume changes feel intentional and smooth
- Audio problems are handled calmly and professionally
- The congregation notices worship, not the technology

---

# At the audio console

## Before the service

- Pray, or take a moment to prepare to serve
- Check in with the Production Lead
- Review the service flow and identify:
  - Worship set
  - Speakers
  - Special elements
  - Videos or media moments
- Confirm the audio system is ready:
  - Inputs are working
  - Microphones are available
  - Wireless systems are functioning
  - Multitrack and playback systems are ready
- Confirm:
  - House microphones are unmuted
  - Streaming audio routing is correct
  - Lobby audio is working
  - All required channels are prepared

## During the service

- Listen actively and make intentional adjustments
- Keep vocals clear and present
- Maintain a balanced worship mix
- Watch for changes in:
  - Worship leaders
  - Musicians
  - Speakers
  - Media playback
- Follow cues from the Production Lead
- Make changes smoothly — avoid distracting adjustments
- Monitor both the room and the online mix throughout

## If something goes wrong

- **Stay calm**
- Identify the problem *before* making major changes
- Check the common issues:
  - Muted channels
  - Incorrect routing
  - Microphone issues
  - Connection problems
- Communicate clearly with the Production Lead
- Make simple, intentional corrections
- Don't panic-adjust several things at once

---

## How to think about success

- The best mix supports worship without drawing attention to itself
- **Clear is better than loud**
- Consistency is better than constant adjustment
- Serve the room, not the equipment

## Team mindset

- Support worship leaders and musicians
- Communicate clearly and respectfully
- Encourage fellow volunteers
- Be teachable and open to feedback
- Production exists to remove distractions and support ministry

## After the service

- Follow the shutdown instructions
- Confirm equipment is properly secured
- Return accessories and equipment to their proper places
- Share any issues or observations with the Production Lead
- Help prepare the system for the next service

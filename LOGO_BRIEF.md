# ProDeck — Logo Design Brief

**Client:** Cornerstone Church, Cheshire — Production/Tech (contact: Zach,
zach@whiteoakmedia.io)
**Deliverable:** Primary logo + app icon for **ProDeck**, with a wordmark lockup.

## What ProDeck is

ProDeck is the church's production control app — a Mac application that sits at
the tech booth and quietly runs Sunday morning: it talks to ProPresenter
(slides/lyrics), Planning Center (the service plan), audio metering/SPL, NDI
camera feeds, MIDI/OSC rigs, NFC giving discs, and drives dashboards on booth
screens, phones, and an office TV. Think "mission control for a worship
service" — always-on, glanceable, operator-grade.

It is an internal tool with a growing footprint: booth operators use it every
week, volunteers see it on phones, staff see a read-only kiosk on an office TV.
It is not a consumer product and never appears in an app store.

## Where the mark will live

| Context | Size reality | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| macOS app icon (Dock, Cmd-Tab, Finder) | 16 px → 1024 px | macOS squircle convention; must survive 16 px |
| In-app brand mark (sidebar) | ~30 px, dark UI | currently a placeholder gradient square |
| Web login card (phones) | ~64 px on dark | first thing volunteers see |
| Kiosk offline splash (office TV) | ~96 px, seen across a room | pairs with "Command Center offline" text |
| Wordmark lockup | header/docs use | "ProDeck" set next to or under the mark |

Dark-first: the app is a permanently dark UI. A light-background variant is
needed only for documents; the mark must be designed on dark and adapted to
light, not vice versa.

## Existing visual language (the mark should live comfortably in this)

- Background `#0e131b` · panel `#161d28` · text `#eef3fa` · muted `#92a2b6`
- Accent blue `#5b8def` (high `#79a4f6`) · success green `#3fcf8e`
- UI style: rounded panels, quiet contrast, small colored status chips; dense
  information presented calmly. Closest aesthetic relatives: Grafana dark,
  Linear, mission-control dashboards.
- The designer may propose an accent evolution, but the logo must not fight
  the existing blue-on-near-black app.

## Tone

Calm, dependable, precise, a bit quietly technical. This is the thing that
does NOT panic on Sunday morning. Avoid: corporate church clichés (crosses,
doves, flames, sound-wave clip-art), gamer RGB energy, gradients-for-the-sake-
of-gradients, anything that looks like a streaming-deck consumer gadget.

Concept territory worth exploring (not prescriptive): decks/surfaces/grids
(the dashboard metaphor), a "P/D" monogram with a control-surface feel, signal
or cue motifs (a beat, a go-cue), the calm-operator idea. One strong simple
idea beats a complex illustration — at 16 px only the silhouette survives.

## Hard requirements

1. **Legible at 16 px** and gorgeous at 1024 px.
2. Works in **one color** (pure white on dark, pure dark on light) with no
   loss of identity — status prints, engraving, favicon fallbacks.
3. Square-safe: the primary mark must sit naturally in the macOS rounded-square
   icon canvas without awkward margins.
4. Wordmark: "ProDeck" (capital P, capital D, one word). Typeface license must
   permit app + web embedding, or be an outlined/custom drawing.
5. No stock icons, no AI-generated raster art as final deliverables — vector
   originals required.

## Deliverables

- **Masters:** layered source (Figma/AI) + clean SVGs: mark alone, wordmark
  alone, horizontal lockup, stacked lockup — each in full-color, all-white,
  all-dark versions.
- **macOS app icon:** 1024×1024 master PNG designed for the squircle mask
  (we generate the .icns/.ico size ramps ourselves: 16, 32, 128, 256, 512,
  1024 + @2x). If the designer wants to hand-tune the 16/32 px sizes, welcome.
- **Web:** 512×512 and 192×192 PNG + a 32×32 favicon-ready simplification.
- Mini usage sheet (one page): clearspace, minimum sizes, do/don't.

## Process

Two concept directions → one round of refinement on the chosen direction →
final files. Decisions by Zach. No hard deadline; quality over speed — this
replaces a placeholder, so it ships when it's right.
